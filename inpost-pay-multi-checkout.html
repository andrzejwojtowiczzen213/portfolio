InPost Pay multi-checkout processIn progress

InPost Pay is the checkout experience for finalising orders in Von Halsky, InPost’s e-commerce platform. The flow includes:

  • Basket list
  • Basket
  • Multi-checkout
  • Payment
  • Transaction history

Von Halsky

InPost is a logistics app used by more than 15 million people in Poland. We recently introduced Von Halsky, an AI shopping assistant inside the app that helps people find products, buy them and track their delivery—from marketplace to doorstep.

My Part
#1 Step: Basket

I worked with the team responsible for designing and implementing the order-finalisation experience. The first step is the basket, where users can select items from different merchants and proceed to multi-checkout.

#2 Step: Multi-checkout

In multi-checkout, users can finalise several carts at once by choosing a delivery method and address for each order, then selecting a payment method.

#3 Step: Post-transaction history and post-sales
